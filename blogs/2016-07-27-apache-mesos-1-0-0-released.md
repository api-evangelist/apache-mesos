Mesos 1.0 release is finally here for download ! As most of you know, Apache Mesos has been running in production at some of the largest Internet companies like Twitter , Netflix , Uber and NASA JPL for many years now. It has been proven to scale to tens of thousands of nodes, running hundreds of thousands of containers serving live traffic without interruption for months at a time.
