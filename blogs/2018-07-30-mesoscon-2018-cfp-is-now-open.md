MesosCon is an annual conference organized by the Apache Mesos community, bringing together users and developers to share and learn about the project and its growing ecosystem. We are excited to announce that MesosCon 2018 will be held in San Francisco on November 5th-7th, 2018. Talk submissions and sponsorship opportunities are now open for the conference!
