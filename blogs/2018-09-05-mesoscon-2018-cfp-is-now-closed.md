Thank you everyone for submitting proposals, our Call For Proposals is now closed. We would like to communicate an important change: MesosCon 2018 will instead be held in the San Francisco area on November 5th-7th, 2018. The dates remain unchanged, but it will not be held in New York City as previously announced.
