We are proud to announce the first ever MesosCon Europe hackathon on Friday, October 9th, 2015. This event is an opportunity to contribute to the project, learn from other members of the community, and build some fantastic hacks. The hackathon is being sponsored by Joyent , and, though not required, participants are invited to use Joyent’s cloud during the hack day.
